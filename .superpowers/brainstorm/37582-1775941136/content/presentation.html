01 — Overview

What we're building

Alienz is a clean, elegant online storefront where customers can browse your collection, save favourites, and place enquiries — with a full admin panel for you to manage everything.

01
Browse & Shop
Visitors can explore your full catalogue, filter by category, and view detailed product pages.
02
Enquiry System
Customers enquire about items directly. You receive their details by email and respond on your terms.
03
Customer Accounts
Registered customers get a personal area with wishlist, order history, and saved address.
04
Admin Panel
Full control over products, categories, enquiries, and customer accounts — from any device.
05
Payment Ready
Built with Stripe in mind. Online payments can be added in a future phase without rebuilding anything.
06
Optimised Images
All product images are automatically compressed and converted to WebP — fast loading on every device.

08 — Roadmap

What comes next

The site launches with enquiry-based ordering. Online payments are planned for Phase 2 — no rebuild required.

Now

Phase 1 — Launch

Full catalogue, customer accounts, wishlist, enquiry system, admin panel. Everything needed to start selling.

Next

Phase 2 — Online Payments

Stripe integration for direct checkout. The existing system is built to accommodate this without any major changes.

Later

Phase 3 — Growth Features

Discount codes, email marketing integration, analytics dashboard, and more — based on what you need.
